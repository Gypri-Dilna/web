# antislop follow-up report 001, 2026-09-10

Follow-up to `audit-001-2026-09-10.md`. All 16 findings were approved for fixing.
Everything below was changed in source. No patch scripts were used and none exist in the
repo.

---

## What changed, by finding

**1. R-02 em dashes.** Removed all 37 (29 in the five pages, 8 in `README.md`), plus 2
found in source comments during verification. Replaced with periods, commas, colons and
parentheses. Every `<title>` now uses a pipe separator. Verified: 0 em dashes across
9 source files.

**2. R-38 invented opening hours.** The "Otevírací doba" block in `kontakt.html` is gone.
It claimed hours that appear in no source. The contact page now says where the workshop
is and what to write about, and makes no claim about when it is open. The README records
that hours are absent on purpose.

**3. R-17 fake statistic.** The `∞` value in the equipment data block is replaced with
"Sponzoři 07", which is a real, countable number. A second instance of the same problem
was found and removed during the work: `nase-prace.html` used four data blocks holding
qualitative words ("Návrh / Modelování", "Výroba / 3D tisk") in the numeric display
treatment. Those blocks are deleted.

**4. R-35 recorded click-through.** Run and recorded below.

**5. R-01 background orb.** The `.glow` element and its two radial gradients are gone.
The page background is flat.

**6. R-07 background layers.** Four layers reduced to one identity motif. Removed: the
`#ambience` canvas, the `.noise` turbulence overlay, and the two full-height hairline
rules in `.hud-frame`. Kept: the corner ticks (they frame content) and the hex-nut chain
divider, which is the logo's own geometry and so is the site's identity rather than
generic texture.

**7. R-06 wide-tracked uppercase.** Letter-spacing dropped across the board. The maximum
in the stylesheet is now **0.1em**, down from 0.24em, and nothing exceeds 0.13em. The
uppercase declaration count fell from 33 to 9 rule groups, and uppercase is now reserved
for functional labels (nav, buttons, data labels, captions) plus display headings.
`card-title`, `timeline-event` and `data-val small` moved to sentence case.

**8. R-14 identical feature cards.** The three-card grid was eliminated in all three
places, and each section was given a composition that matches what its content is:
- `index.html` "Co u nás děláme" is now asymmetric: 3D printing is the lead item (large
  icon, full text, top rule), electronics and programming are compact icon-plus-text rows.
  The hierarchy now says what the workshop's headline capability is.
- `cansat.html` "Jak soutěž probíhá" is now prose. The three-step card shape was the
  "How It Works always 3 steps" tell, and the page already has a real four-phase timeline
  further down, so the cards were also redundant.
- `nase-prace.html` "Speciální komponenty" was folded into the section above it as prose,
  removing a section that existed to fill a template (C-3).
- `index.html` "Co jsme vyrobili" changed from four identical cards to a link list.

**9. R-19 motion.** The reveal system is deleted entirely: 59 `.reveal` elements, the
`IntersectionObserver`, the `.js` gating class and its inline head script are all gone.
The endless ambience loop went with the canvas. `main.js` is now 32 lines and does one
thing: the mobile nav. MOTION is declared 1 in `DESIGN.md`.

**10. R-13 / R-29 accent spread.** Mint is now limited to exactly five roles: primary
action, current-page marker, focus ring, text selection, and the hex divider. Removed
from section indices, eyebrows, tech titles, timeline dates, data values, callout
backgrounds and hover states. Two accent stripes were removed because they were the
"colored left stripe" tell: the `.callout` left border and the `.feature-lead` top
border. Hover feedback is now border weight and underline instead of colour.

**11. R-05 section rhythm.** With finding 8's restructure, the five index sections no
longer share one composition. RHYTHM is declared 2 in `DESIGN.md`.

**12. R-08 arrows.** All `↗` characters removed. External links are no longer
distinguished by a decorative arrow.

**13. R-05 footer.** The three-column footer is replaced by a two-part structure: a brand
block with the contact address, and a single inline nav of the links the site actually
has. On mobile those links become 44px rows.

**14. R-31 written reasons.** `DESIGN.md` now records the reason for colour, accent,
theme, typography, uppercase use, layout, cards, icons, radius, whitespace and the hex
divider.

**15. R-21 dark theme.** Documented as a deliberate choice in `DESIGN.md`, with the
reason: the workshop is a technical space and the reference project named by the owner is
dark. Cloud Paper still does real work as the text colour and as the sponsor card
background. If you want a light/dark toggle, that is a separate piece of work.

**16. Dials and Design Read.** Both now declared at the top of `DESIGN.md`:
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Not done, and why

**R-21 light/dark toggle.** The dark theme is documented as a deliberate choice rather
than left unexplained, which satisfies the rule's honesty requirement. A toggle is a
real feature with real work behind it, not a cleanup item, so it is left as a decision
for you. The brand guide describes Cloud Paper as the base that "zajišťuje lehkost a
čitelnost", so if the site should be light-first, that is a direction change worth making
deliberately.

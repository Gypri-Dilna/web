# Website audit, 29 October 2026

Reviewed all five pages on the current interaction branch, including section screenshots
at 390px and 1440px, mobile and desktop navigation, video, and enlarged text.

## Fixed

- Contact and sponsor heroes had a trailing 2rem paragraph margin in addition to their
  bottom padding. Apply the action-row gap only when another element follows the lead.
  The sponsor support heading now uses the shared section-heading gap.
- The mobile menu's close symbol lost its horizontal centering transform when rotated.
- Photo focus outlines extended outside overflow-clipped media wells. Keep them inset.
- Without JavaScript, mobile navigation covered the hero. If main.js failed to load,
  the early inline `.js` class also left reveals hidden. Enable enhancement after setup
  and keep fallback navigation in the document flow.
- The 1286×720 report video was cropped into the default 16:10 photo frame. Give it a
  matching video well and use `object-fit: contain` to preserve the whole picture.
- Team card sizes used pixel limits, so enlarged captions could give cards unequal
  widths. Use rem limits, name the keyboard-focusable strip, and explain horizontal
  scrolling while retaining the existing hidden scrollbar treatment.
- Enlarged contact handles could extend past the viewport. Allow emergency wrapping.
- Contact copy and metadata retained the older club schedule. Match the current homepage:
  usually twice monthly, Wednesdays from 14:00. Normalize the homepage time formatting.
- CanSat copy rendered Markdown asterisks and repeated unsupported superlatives. Use HTML
  emphasis and describe the workshop's concrete contribution. Correct the homepage's
  Alliance Laundry Systems spelling and the missing space in “Gypri dílně”.
- Shared stylesheet/script versions differed between pages. Use v4 together on all pages
  so newly served HTML cannot mix the old photo viewer with the new stylesheet.

## Verification

- Chromium and WebKit checks cover 320, 390, 520, 768, 900, 1440 and 2560px, text overflow,
  photo focus, hero margins, video dimensions and team card sizing at 200% text.
- The dependency-free `/checks/interactions.html` covers menu and photo-dialog behavior,
  team keyboard access, and a sandboxed script-free homepage.
- Additional checks block main.js and remove IntersectionObserver to exercise fallbacks.
- Design-guard passes all five production pages at 100/100. Syntax and diff checks pass.
- Local assets, page links, fragment targets and IDs were checked; none were missing or
  duplicated. Script and stylesheet versions match across the five pages.
